Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This document explains how ForgeProject (forgeproject.tech) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our academic portal, project downloads, and online tools.

1. Overview & Consent

At ForgeProject, accessible from https://forgeproject.tech, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information recorded by ForgeProject and how we utilize it.

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our website. It uses cookies, commonly known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visit to forgeproject.tech and other websites across the Internet.

Important Disclosure Regarding Google Advertising

• Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
• Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the web.
• Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
• Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Some of our advertising partners may also use cookies and web beacons on our site. Each of our advertising partners has its own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data. For easier access, you can read Google's Advertising Policies here: Google Advertising Policies & Technologies.

3. Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We use Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-CZX1KYVMT3) to gather aggregated, anonymized insights regarding user engagement, page visits, and file download events (e.g., project ZIP kit downloads, PPT deck views, Viva prep bot sessions).

Google Analytics collects data such as browser type, operating system, referring URL, time spent on pages, and approximate geographic location (city level). This data is purely analytical and does not identify individual visitors personally. You can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your activity across the web by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Log Files

ForgeProject follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

5. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

Like any other website, ForgeProject uses 'cookies' and browser localStorage. These are used to store information including:

  • Theme Preferences: Saving your Dark / Light mode choice across sessions.
  • Saved Projects Library: Storing bookmarked project cards in your local browser storage for quick reference without requiring an account.
  • AI Customizer Studio Inputs: Temporarily saving student names and supervisor titles to generate your customized PPTs locally.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

6. CCPA & GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights under international data regulations (including GDPR and CCPA):

Right to Access

You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.

Right to Erasure

Right to Rectification

You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.

Right to Opt-Out

7. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

ForgeProject does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
