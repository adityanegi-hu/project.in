3. Frequently Asked Questions

Are all projects on ForgeProject free?

Yes. 100% of the project source code, PPT presentation decks, synopsis documents, and AI defense guides are free to download and use for academic study.

How do I run the downloaded code?

Every generated ZIP kit includes a comprehensive setup guide (README.md or SETUP.txt) with step-by-step terminal commands for installing dependencies.

Can I customize the student & guide name on PPTs?

Yes! Use our in-browser AI PPT Studio on the homepage to personalize author names, supervisor credentials, and university details before downloading.

How does the AI Viva Bot work?

The Viva Bot simulates questions asked by external university examiners, evaluates your answers technically, and gives you a score and tips to improve your defense.
